Canadian Multinational
Company: Alimentation Couche-Tard Inc.
Based in: Laval, Québec, Canada

Description of goods/services:
- Alimentation Couche-Tard Inc. is a Canadian multinational retail leader in the convenience store and road transportation fuel sectors
- Operates thousands of physical retail locations offering fresh food, prepared quick-service meals, hot and cold beverages, packaged snacks, and everyday household essentials
- Distributes and sells fuel (gasoline and diesel) under Circle K, Couche-Tard, Shell, and Esso banners in Canada

Countries the company has done business with:
- Operates over 17,300 stores across 27 countries and territories worldwide
- North America: Over 7,200 stores in the U.S. across 48 states, 1188 stores in Mexico, and around 2,100 stores in Canada with coast-to-coast coverage
- Eurasia: Operates in Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Germany, Poland, Ireland, Belgium, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Baltic states, and Hong Kong
- Over 2,700 franchised stores in other nations such as Saudi Arabia, Egypt, UAE, Vietnam, Guatemala, and New Zealand

Type of international business the company has been involved with:
- Primary driver is Foreign Direct Investment (FDI), acquiring entire foreign retail chains, fuel networks, and real estate assets directly (i.e., Circle K in the US, Statoil in Europe, TotalEnergies assets in Western Europe)
- Licenses the Circle K brand name, operating systems, and store blueprints to local independent operators in regions where corporate ownership isn't used
- Imports global private-label goods, specialty food items, and beverage inventory to supply retail network
- Partners with major oil refiners (Shell, Esso) and digital delivery platforms (DoorDash, Uber Eats) to maximize traffic and sales reach

The industry the company operates in:
- Tertiary industry (service and retail sector)
- Functions as a retail distributor and service provider, selling finished consumer goods, convenience meals, mobility fuels, and financial services directly to consumers
- Uses primary industries to extract raw commodities and secondary industries to refine crude oil

Does the company have branch plants in other countries:
- Maintains 15 corporate business units in the U.S., 3 in Canada, a major European head office in Oslo, Norway, and localized operational management teams throughout Europe and Asia
- Owns and operates foreign fuel terminals, bulk storage facilities, transport trucking fleets, and localized regional distribution centers to keep its retail store inventory supplied

The company’s competitive advantages:
- Leverages a network of over 17,300 stores to secure lower wholesale inventory and fuel purchase costs from global vendors
- Uses the recognizable Circle K brand globally (outside of Quebec), building high familiarity with international consumers and travelers
- Often acquires underperforming convenience chains and reconstructs them into high-margin assets using a decentralized management strategy
- Pioneered Electric Vehicle (EV) fast-charging hubs early in Norway
